The math

An estimate, not a guess

The app uses Epley. Brzycki is here for comparison: for the same set it lands a little lower. Both are most reliable at 10 reps or fewer.

Epley

Used in the app

1RM = W × (1 + R / 30)

140 kg × 5 reps163.3 kg

Brzycki

For comparison

1RM = W × 36 / (37 − R)

140 kg × 5 reps157.5 kg

W is the weight lifted and R the reps performed.

FAQ

Questions from the floor

What is a 1RM (one rep max)?

Your one rep max (1RM) is the most weight you can lift for a single repetition of an exercise with good form. It is the standard measure of maximal strength in powerlifting and weightlifting, and the number most strength programs are written against.

How do you estimate 1RM without maxing out?

Estimation formulas predict your 1RM from a set you can already do: the weight and the reps. The Epley formula is 1RM = W × (1 + R / 30), so 140 kg for 5 reps estimates 163.3 kg. Estimates are most reliable at 10 reps or fewer; above that, the app reminds you they are less accurate.

Which formula does the app use?

Epley. It is the most widely used formula in the strength community. Brzycki gives slightly lower numbers for the same set; at low reps the two stay close. No formula replaces a tested max, but an estimate is enough to set your working weights.

What working weights does it give me?

From your best 1RM, the weight for every percentage from 100% down to 50% in 5% steps, or what you should manage for 1 to 10 reps. Weights are rounded to plates you can actually load: 1 kg or 5 lb.

Do I need an account or a connection?

No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your phone, and the app works without a connection.
